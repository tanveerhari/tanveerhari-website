I Won't Come Back

When you can see The sun shine in the east, You’d know that I m gone, Not to turn back, as I won’t come back.

I won’t shed a tear In the light. Though at night, Won’t let them stop; yet, I won’t come back.

Can’t imagine this life, Without you, but ‘m Ready for this hell; as Not even my ashes would return, for I won’t come back.

When your heart will beat For me, as your thoughts Wander around me; I will haunt your dreams, but I won’t come back.
