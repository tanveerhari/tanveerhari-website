For My Love

Baby, the following verse, Is exclusively for you; For I can’t forget you, And am dying to meet you.

Love makes you desperate to fall in, Then takes you as high as heaven, Just to be lost, leaving you distressed. And distressed I am, to lose you.

Fool I was, to let you go. Then broke all the bonds, So as not be able to reach you, Just to be desperate to want you, again.

Why on earth did I say good bye? Why didn’t I stop you? As you went away, Far from my reach.

And this weather just helps, In making me more frantic, As I miss you more, I want you baby, above everything.

Damn, those eyes, Beautiful and patient, Filled with love for me; I miss them.

Not your words, But your manner of saying them, Your sweet smile, filled with innocence, I can’t forget.

Your warmth besides me, In those cold months Of bitterness, I am impatient to feel.

God! I want those nights back, With you holding my hand, Among those many stars. That heaven, I desire again.

Why couldn’t it all be Just, but a painful dream? Why can’t I just open my eyes, And find your love greeting me?

Now can’t hold back these tears, As they escape my eyes; can’t pretend Anymore to be cool without you, Cause I’m incomplete without you.

May this verse reach you. May it ever make it to your eyes, Or fall to your ears. May it bring you back, to me.
