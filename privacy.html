Privacy

No cookies. No tracking.

This is a personal website. It is built to be read, not to collect anything about the people who read it.

Cookies

This site sets no cookies, of any kind. There is nothing to accept or decline.

Analytics and tracking

There are no analytics, advertising or social-media scripts, no tracking pixels and no fingerprinting. Nobody is building a profile of your visit.

Third parties

Every file, including the fonts, is served from tanveerhari.com itself. Pages make no requests to other companies while you read them. Links to LinkedIn, GitHub or Mviaso take you to sites with their own privacy policies.

What your browser stores

If you close the small privacy notice, your browser remembers that in its local storage (one entry, privacy-notice), so the notice does not appear again. It stays on your device and is never sent anywhere. Clearing this site's data removes it.

Hosting

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web server, GitHub may record technical data such as IP addresses to keep the service secure; see the GitHub Privacy Statement. Tanveer does not receive or use these logs.

Email

If you write to tanveerhari@gmail.com, your address and message are used only to reply, and are never shared or added to any list.

Changes

If analytics are ever added, they will be privacy-friendly and cookieless, or you will be asked first, and this page will say so. Last updated 7 October 2026.
