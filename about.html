About

Tanveer Singh ਤਨਵੀਰ ਸਿੰਘ

[PORTRAIT: on a walk]

Tanveer Singh is a software architect and writer, and co-founder and CTO of Mviaso, a company that studies wisdom, in minds and in machines, and builds AI that grows wiser alongside the people it serves.

Born in Chandigarh in 1988, he grew up on Guru Nanak’s and Kabir’s stories; what stayed was their simplicity. Programming arrived in 2003, a career in software in 2010, and domain languages in 2016. The poems that survive begin in 2007, and they never quite stopped.

Away from work: history and philosophy, long walks, the night sky, chess and the odd grand-strategy game.
