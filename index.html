Approach

Software as creative expression.

Close to sixteen years of architecting software across consumer goods, ecommerce, manufacturing, telecom and QA automation, at the meeting point of Domain-Driven Design, DSL development, and applied research in ML, AI and IoT.

Programming is a kind of writing, and well-designed systems read like stories. The expertise is not in mastering languages; it is in understanding problems deeply and crafting solutions that serve the people who eventually use them.

The work is rooted in systems thinking that reaches beyond code: organisational design, team dynamics and behavioural psychology are part of every architectural decision.

Simplicity

The simplest expression of an idea is usually the most durable.

Collaboration

The best architectures emerge from teams that elevate each other.

Craftsmanship

Frameworks are tools, not destinations. Slow, deliberate work outlasts trends.
